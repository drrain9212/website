About Me

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Missouri–Kansas City. My research focuses on the security of AI systems, with an emphasis on adversarial machine learning, speech processing, and audio watermarking.

Prospective Students: I am interested in working with motivated Ph.D. students on AI security and related topics.
